# Orindle brand guidelines

## Brand idea
Orindle makes a complex, fast-changing AI landscape feel like one calm, capable workspace. Personality: considered, clear, quietly confident, useful, human. Avoid noisy “AI magic” shorthand and inflated promises.

## Name and voice
- Always write **Orindle** in sentence case; wordmark may use lowercase `orindle`.
- Voice: direct, warm, specific. Explain a capability, limitation or next action in ordinary language.
- Prefer “compatible models,” “standalone HTML,” “monthly credits,” and “demonstration” when accurate.
- Never claim guaranteed output quality, unrestricted/unlimited access, partnerships, model count or privacy properties without evidence.

## Tagline and claim gate
Requested commercial tagline: **One Workspace. 100+ AI Models.** Use only when the live catalogue proves >=100 unique active chat-capable model IDs after aliases are deduplicated. Until verified, use **One workspace for your AI workflow.** The current preview uses a nonnumeric brand line to avoid an unsubstantiated count.

## Mark
The prototype symbol is an original orbital/connected-circle motif in emerald, paired with a Poppins wordmark. It symbolizes an interconnected toolset without depicting a provider logo. Keep the mark simple and clear at small sizes; do not add provider brand marks to it. The implementation uses Lucide `Orbit` as a provisional mark; replace with a commissioned trademark-cleared vector after identity review.

## Color and typography
- White is dominant. Green means action, selection, success or an intentional accent.
- Palette: ink `#111512`; green `#16865B`; forest `#10271E`; mint `#DDF5E9`; cyan `#DDF5F8`; neutral backgrounds and borders as in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
- Poppins 400/500/600/700, locally served through `next/font/local` for optimized loading.
- Avoid purple/neon gradients, broad glass effects, heavy shadows and all-over gradients.

## Imagery and demonstrations
Prefer real, current product UI. Before real product capture exists, use original purpose-built UI and label it as a demo. Never invent testimonials, avatars, user counts, popularity rankings, earnings, provider endorsements or generation results that might be understood as actual customer activity.

## Copy examples
- Good: “Model availability depends on provider support and current catalogue access.”
- Good: “Standalone HTML website. Demo preview.”
- Avoid: “The world’s best AI,” “unlimited everything,” “trusted by thousands,” or a numeric catalogue count without verification.
