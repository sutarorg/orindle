# Competitor research notes

**Evidence note:** no live competitor-site crawl was possible under the network constraints for this coding run. This is a research framework and product-pattern synthesis based on the requested reference set; it intentionally avoids pretending that current pages, pricing, features or mobile states were observed. Revalidate before product decisions or publication.

## Reference set and lenses
- [AI Fiesta](https://aifiesta.ai/) — use as a reference for the long landing-page storytelling composition specified in the brief. Inspect the current page order, app preview, provider/model proof, offer clarity, FAQs and CTA transitions. Rebuild the narrative with Orindle’s own visual grammar.
- [Poe](https://poe.com/) — inspect model/bot browsing, selection and usage communication; note how discovery density is balanced with conversation.
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) — inspect the recognizable prompt-first interaction, history, responsive composer and trust cues; do not clone branded details.
- [Monica AI](https://monica.im/) — inspect broad-assistant information architecture and how cross-workflow functionality is explained without making the product feel crowded.
- [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/) — inspect catalog metadata and provider transparency; distinguish a developer router/catalogue from a polished end-user workspace.
- Specialist references: image/video generation interfaces and search assistants should be evaluated for controls, progress feedback, provenance and export patterns.

## Comparative product hypotheses (not current-state assertions)

| Primary mental model | Choose among bots/models | Choose model/provider/route | Ask a general assistant | Assistant across many contexts | One workspace with five clear destination tools |

| Marketing proof | Model variety | Breadth and transparency | Familiar interaction | Reach and convenience | Product screenshot, supported capability details, one transparent plan |

## Landing-page pattern findings to test
The composition in the brief is effective as a narrative hypothesis because it answers successive questions: What is it? What does it look like? What can it connect to? What can I do? What does it cost? Is it for me? What else is included? What concerns should I resolve? What is the next action? The implementation puts the product preview immediately after the value proposition and before model inventory, then moves through capability, price and reassurance. It avoids fabricated testimonials and uses use cases in their place.

## Do not copy
Do not reuse competitors’ logos, wordmarks, screenshots, exact text, color signatures, custom illustrations or distinctive layout details. Provider names are plain-text identifiers only; they do not imply partnership. Orindle’s orbital mark, green/mint palette, Poppins typography and original demo agency preview are locally created.

## Validation checklist for a later live audit
For each reference: page section order; first fold; nav and mobile nav; model/provider naming; plan dimensions and billing caveats; trial/cancellation language; prompt interaction; empty/loading/error states; mobile feature access; keyboard/focus behavior; privacy and content ownership statements; evidence for testimonials and counts. Record URL, access date, viewport, screenshots and confidence. Distinguish a first-hand observation from an inference.
